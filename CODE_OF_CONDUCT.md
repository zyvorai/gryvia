# Code of conduct

Gryvia follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
(CC BY 4.0). In short:

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and welcoming; assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the community.
- Own mistakes, apologise, and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when representing the project in
public. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or
permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem inappropriate.

To report a problem, use GitHub's private reporting on this repository (Security > Report a vulnerability, and
say it is a conduct report) so that it reaches the maintainers without being public. Reports are handled
confidentially and fairly.
